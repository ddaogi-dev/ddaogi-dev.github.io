# Tankbook: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Tankbook ("the app") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

## Data you enter

Vehicles, fill-ups, service records, expenses and settings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer cannot see them.

## Location

The app does not request or use your location.

## Backups and exports

When you export a backup (JSON) or a spreadsheet (CSV) file, the app saves it to the location you pick in the system file picker, such as your device storage or Google Drive. The file is then handled by that location's provider under its own terms. Restoring reads a file you pick and replaces the data on your device.

## Notifications

Service reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

## Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases. If an optional one-time upgrade is added later, it will be processed by Google Play, the developer will not receive your payment details, and this policy will be updated before that release.

## Permissions

- Notifications: to show service reminders.
- Run at startup: to restore reminders after the device restarts.
- Network access and network state: added by a Google Play library included in the app. The app itself sends no data over the network.
- Vibration: for reminder notifications.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files beyond the ones you pick in the system file picker.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all data on your device. Backup files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
