# Bill Reminder: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Bill Reminder ("the app") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

## Data you enter

Bills, payments, pay schedules and settings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer cannot see them.

## Backups and exports

When you export a backup (JSON) or a CSV file, the app saves it to the location you pick in the system file picker, such as your device storage or Google Drive. The file is then handled by that location's provider under its own terms. Restoring reads a file you pick and replaces the data on your device.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

## Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether Pro has been purchased. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles purchases.

## Permissions

- Notifications: to show bill reminders.
- Run at startup: to restore reminders after the device restarts.
- Billing and network access: required by Google Play's billing library for the Pro purchase. The app itself sends no data over the network.
- Vibration: for reminder notifications.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or files beyond the ones you pick.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all data on your device. Backup files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
