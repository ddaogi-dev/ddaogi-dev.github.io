# Sudoku Steps: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Sudoku Steps ("the app") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

Your games, notes, stats and settings stay on your device. There is no account and no analytics. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

## Data you create in the app

Games in progress, finished-game records, stats and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer cannot see them. Puzzles are built into the app; playing them needs no internet connection.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner at the top of the screen, and a rewarded ad only if you choose to watch one for an extra hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

- your device's advertising ID,
- your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location,
- device and app information (such as device model, operating system and app version),
- how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads use your data for personalization. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings.

## Backups

When you export a backup (JSON file), the app saves it to the location you pick in the system file picker, such as your device storage or Google Drive. The file is then handled by that location's provider under its own terms. Restoring reads a file you pick and replaces the data in the app.

## Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases. If an optional upgrade is added later, it will be processed by Google Play, the developer will not receive your payment details, and this policy will be updated before that release.

## Permissions

- Network access and network state: to load ads. The game itself works offline.
- Advertising ID and ad services: used by Google AdMob as described above.
- Keep awake and foreground service: added by a Google library that the ads SDK uses for short background tasks.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files beyond the ones you pick in the system file picker.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all game data on your device. Backup files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy above.
